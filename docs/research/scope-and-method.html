Research scope and method

This public wiki is generated from the companion identity-fraud research repository. Its purpose is to make reviewed, cited guidance easy to use without publishing personal case details.

Evidence rules

  • Primary sources come first: federal agencies, statutes and regulations, and controlling insurer/provider documents.
  • Marketing claims can identify a feature or price, but cannot establish what an insurance policy covers.
  • Provider conclusions must distinguish monitoring, restoration assistance, and insurance reimbursement.
  • Coverage findings require the actual policy, certificate, summary of benefits, exclusions, and state-specific terms where applicable.
  • Every material claim is access-dated and linked to its source.

Research status

The current public baseline covers official free recovery and credit-file tools. No provider is recommended yet. The unresolved question with the highest impact is how each insurer and restoration program treats a suspicious incident that was known before enrollment.
