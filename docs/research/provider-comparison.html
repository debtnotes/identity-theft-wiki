Read this table correctly

This is an evidence map, not a rating or product endorsement. “Located” means a current official provider page or summary was found on August 18, 2026; it does not mean the terms apply to every plan, state, or consumer.

Comparison rule

The best candidate is not necessarily the plan with the biggest advertised limit. It is the plan, if any, whose exact state-specific policy provides the desired future-event protection and restoration service at an acceptable price, after existing coverage and account controls are considered.
